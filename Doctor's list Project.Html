• Our Doctors

    • Dr. Linda Malson

      Dr. Linda Malson is a board-certified dermatologist. She graduated magna cum laude from Brown University with a Bachelor of Science in Biology and Community Health. She returned to Brown to complete her medical education at the Warren Alpert Medical School where she was inducted into the prestigious Alpha Omega Alpha Honor Medical Society. She then completed a rigorous Internal Medicine Internship at the Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine followed by her Dermatology Residency at Howard University. In her free time, Dr. Malson enjoys going for long walks with her two golden retrievers and boating on the lake and river on the weekends.

    • Dr. Eny Queatan

      Dr. Eny Queatan is a board-certified dermatologist joining IDI from her practice at NorthShore University Health System. Born and raised in Highland Park, Illinois, Dr. Eny found her passion for medicine and dermatology while serving as a clinical dermatology assistant prior to college. At the University of Wisconsin in Madison, she graduated with distinction, then pursued her medical training at the Chicago Medical School at Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine and Science. After medical school, Dr. Queatan completed her general medicine internship at Advocate Lutheran General Hospital. She then completed her residency in dermatology at SUNY Downstate in Brooklyn, NY. Dr. Eny enjoys caring for patients with a variety of dermatologic conditions. She values a thorough approach, working closely with patients to achieve the best clinical outcomes.

    • Dr. Stephan Gorge

      Received Dermatology and Dermasurgery Board Certification Born and raised in Ohio, Benjamin Stephan, MD, graduated Phi Beta Kappa from the University of Michigan. He was awarded the William Barnstrom Freshman Award for Academic Achievement and was selected as a James B. Angell Scholar. Dr. Stephan received his MD degree from the University of Michigan Medical School. His Dermatology residency specialty training was completed at the University of Chicago Pritzker School of Medicine. He received his Dermatology and Dermasurgery Board Certification. Dr. Stephan is presently an Assistant Professor of Clinical Dermatology at Northwestern University Medical School. At the Medical School, he teaches Dermatology and Cosmetic Skin Surgery to medical students, interns, and surgical residents. Professional memberships and fellowships include the American Academy of Dermatology, American Academy of Cosmetic Surgery, American Society of Dermatologic Surgery, American Medical Association, Illinois State Medical Society, Chicago Medical Society, Chicago Dermatologic Society, and the American Professional Practice Association. He is the author or co-author of twelve scientific journal articles, a medical textbook chapter, and has been a reviewer for the Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology and the Journal of the American Medical Association. Dr. Stephan has served as a guest expert on Oprah, The Today Show, The Phil Donahue Show, and ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC News Programs.

    • Dr. Mariya Bonafatte

      Mariya is a Dermatology Board-Certified Nurse Practitioner with over 12 years of experience caring for dermatology patients of all ages. She strives to make every patient feel comfortable, fully understand the custom plan of care and answer all of their questions. Mariya has extensive experience and a passion for the treatment of many medical skin conditions including acne, eczema, psoriasis, hair loss and rashes. She specializes in the detection and treatment of skin cancer. With years of training in facial rejuvenation, Mariya uses injectable fillers, neurotoxins, and custom skincare regimens to help her patients maintain and restore their natural beauty. When not in the office, Mariya enjoys travel, kayaking and camping with her two sons as well as working with area animal rescues.
